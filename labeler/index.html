Why these labels, and what happens with them

Each cell is one 10 m satellite pixel. The fall-colour map decides, per pixel, whether there is a deciduous tree and how far its colour has turned. Your labels are the reference the map is scored against: half are used to retune the colour rules, the other half to report the map's precision and recall. Labels are stored per cell with your account id and a timestamp; the map's own prediction for each cell is deliberately hidden here.

Conventions: a conifer with a deciduous tree at the edge is "Conifer" with cover "Edge only". A lawn with a single small ornamental is "Lawn". Brown or bare crowns count as deciduous turning if leaves remain, "Built / bare" if the crown is empty.
